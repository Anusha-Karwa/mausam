# The nine checks

| # | Check | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Problem real? | **YES** | Monthly WhatsApp screenshots to a CA; can't tell market vs fund moves. |
| 02 | Workflow repeated? | **YES** | Every weekend + monthly check-ins; grows with each new fund. |
| 03 | Input available? | **PARTIAL** | NAVs (mfapi.in, with AMFI NAVAll.txt as fallback), index levels (Yahoo, with proxy fallback), news (GNews/RSS) available; official benchmark via mapping + override; category average computed from peers; real transaction execution not available (cut). |
| 04 | Output valuable? | **YES** | Weekly 3-sentence per-fund explanations + one verdict with a reason. |
| 05 | Impact measurable? | **YES** | On-time reports, % of moves attributed, time-to-understand, CA messages, forecast skill vs naive. |
| 06 | Failure risk OK? | **YES with guardrails** | Numbers in code, number-check, fixed verdicts, no advice, ranges not prices, stale flags, disclaimer. |
| 07 | Judgment protected? | **YES** | Explains, never transacts; forecasts never drive verdicts; decisions stay with Meera (and her adviser). |
| 08 | ROI worth it? | **YES** | ₹0/month on free tiers; replaces a manual monthly process; scales to any number of funds. |
| 09 | Owner clear? | **YES** | Meera owns her fund list, transactions and decisions; automated jobs own data and reports; CA friend is the escalation point. |

**The cut:** executing real buy/sell orders, and single-number price predictions (replaced by ranges).
