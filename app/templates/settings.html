Alert thresholds

These decide when Mausam calls a fund "lagging", when a move counts as "with the market", and when your mix has drifted. The defaults suit most people.

Advanced: change thresholds

Numbers are in {{ term('points', 'points') }}: the gap between two percentages. If your fund returned −2% and its benchmark −1%, the gap is 1 point.
