About Mausam

Mausam means "weather" in Hindi. It's a plain-language weekly weather report on your mutual funds: what happened, why, and whether anything needs your attention.

The problem: Meera's monthly WhatsApp

Meera is 28 and not a finance professional. For three years she has run five monthly {{ term('sip', 'SIPs') }}: a large-cap index fund, a mid-cap fund, a small-cap fund, a short-duration debt fund and an international {{ term('fof', 'fund of funds') }}.

Her fund app shows only absolute returns and {{ term('nav', 'NAV') }}. It can't tell her whether a fund is doing well compared with its {{ term('benchmark', 'benchmark') }} and similar funds, or whether a bad week was the whole market falling or a problem with her fund.

So once a month she screenshots her NAVs and sends them to a CA friend on WhatsApp with one question: "Is this okay?"

That question deserves a calm, clear answer every week, without jargon and without waiting for a friend's free time.

What Mausam does

  • Fetches every fund's NAV daily from India's public mutual fund data.
  • Works out 1-week, 1-month, {{ term('ytd', 'year-to-date') }}, 1-year and 3-year returns in code, never by AI.
  • Compares each fund with its benchmark index and its {{ term('category average', 'category average') }}, and labels each week's move "market-wide" or "fund-specific".
  • Every Saturday morning, Gemini Flash writes a three-sentence summary per fund and one verdict: "No action needed", "One fund worth watching" or "Consider reviewing your allocation".
  • Saves every weekly snapshot so you can see trends over months.
  • Lets you add or remove a fund by typing its scheme code. No code changes.
  • Estimates a likely NAV range for the next 7 and 30 days with a statistical model, and shows honestly how accurate it has been.

The seven flows

  1. Daily NAV jobVercel Cron (with a secret) loads your funds, fetches the latest NAVs from mfapi.in and 5-day index closes from Yahoo (or an index-fund backup), checks for stale data and big moves, and asks Gemini for a guardrail-checked daily tip.Every night, about 23:30 IST
  2. Forecasts and category averagesGitHub Actions loads at least 300 NAVs per fund, refreshes peer-fund category averages, fits the hybrid ARIMA + Ridge model, backtests it against a naive guess, calibrates the ranges and asks Gemini for a two-sentence explanation.Friday night, about 01:00 IST Saturday, or on demand
  3. Saturday weather reportRe-fetches Friday's NAVs, index moves and news; computes returns, snapshots and attribution; the rule-based verdict engine picks the verdict; Gemini writes the report from a facts sheet; guardrails check it (or a plain template is used); then it is saved and emailed.Saturday, about 07:30 IST, or "Generate now"
  4. Add or remove a fundYou type a name or scheme code; Mausam previews it from mfapi.in, maps its category to an asset class and benchmark, then saves it, backfills its NAV history and rebuilds past snapshots. Removing is a soft delete.Whenever you use Manage funds
  5. Your daily visitEach page reads NAVs, transactions, snapshots, reports, forecasts, tips and alerts, then works out units, value, gain, {{ term('xirr', 'XIRR') }}, allocation and returns against benchmark and category.Every time you open the app
  6. Logging a buy, sell or SIPYou invest in your real app, then log it here. Mausam finds that day's NAV (next business day if it was a holiday), works out units, checks you aren't selling more than you hold, and updates your value and XIRR.On the Transactions page
  7. Ask Mausam chatYour question plus a summary of your portfolio and the latest report (no name or email) goes to Gemini. Guardrails block advice, promises and off-topic answers before you see the reply.Up to 20 questions an hour

Where the data comes from, and its limits

Mausam uses live, free, public data. Free data has gaps, so here is exactly what we do about each one.

TopicWhat Mausam does
NAVsFrom mfapi.in. NAVs are published once per business day after markets close, so they are not real-time. If mfapi.in fails or is behind, the nightly job falls back to AMFI's official daily NAV file. If the latest NAV is more than 4 days old on a weekday, the fund is marked stale everywhere and in the report.
Index levelsFrom Yahoo Finance's public chart data. If Yahoo fails or blocks us, Mausam measures the index through the NAV of an index fund that tracks it, and labels it "via index fund". International indices are converted to rupee returns using USD/INR.
Benchmark choiceNo free source gives a fund's official benchmark, so Mausam maps it from the fund's category (large cap → Nifty 50, mid cap → Nifty Midcap 150, and so on). You can see how it was chosen and override it on Manage funds.
Small-cap fundsFree daily history for the Nifty Smallcap 100 isn't available, so small-cap funds are compared with the Nifty Smallcap 250, which is the official benchmark of most small-cap funds anyway.
Category averageNot an official figure. Mausam computes it from up to 12 named similar funds (direct plan, growth option, same category) and always shows how many were used.
Price vs total returnIndex figures are price-index returns, while fund NAVs include reinvested dividends. That gives funds a small built-in edge (total-return indices aren't freely available).
Debt fund benchmarksOfficial CRISIL debt indices aren't free, so debt funds are compared with their category average only.
NewsHeadlines from GNews, with Economic Times and Moneycontrol RSS feeds as a backup. Mausam shows only the headline, source, date and link, plus its own one-line "why it matters". If nothing relevant is found, the report says so rather than guessing.
{# ---- Decisions ---- #}

Design decisions

Neon instead of Supabase

The brief mentions Supabase; Mausam deliberately uses Neon. Both are hosted PostgreSQL. Neon gives serverless Postgres with a native Vercel integration, branching, and auto-suspend on the free plan. The schema is plain SQL, so it is portable to Supabase in minutes.

Which Gemini model

All AI writing (reports, tips, chat and forecast explanations) uses Gemini Flash. The brief named gemini-2.5-flash, but it is no longer available to new API keys, so Mausam uses gemini-3.8-flash. The model is set by the GEMINI_MODEL environment variable, so it can be changed without touching code.

Forecasts are statistics, not AI

The forecasting model is a hybrid ARIMA (AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average) + Ridge Regression. That is statistical modelling run in GitHub Actions, not an AI service. It shows ranges, is tested against a simple "stays the same" guess every week, and never affects the verdict.

Numbers come from code

Every number in a report is calculated in Python first. Gemini only writes sentences around a facts sheet, and code checks the draft: three sentences per fund, a verdict that matches the rules, no invented numbers and no buy/sell phrases. If a draft fails twice, a plain template is used instead.

{# ---- Privacy ---- #}

Privacy

Mausam never sends your name or email address to Gemini. The AI sees only fund names, numbers computed by the app, news headlines and your question. Mausam uses Gemini's free tier, and Google may use free-tier prompts to improve its products, so please don't type personal details into Ask Mausam.

All keys and passwords live in environment variables, never in the code. You can download everything Mausam stores about you from Settings.

Mausam is an educational tool, not investment advice. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks.
