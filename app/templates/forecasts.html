Model card

Educational estimate, not a guarantee or advice
Data used
Daily {{ term('nav', 'NAV') }} history for this fund from mfapi.in (up to 3 years), plus its {{ term('benchmark', 'benchmark') }} and {{ term('category average', 'category average') }} returns as extra clues. No news, no opinions.
Method
Hybrid ARIMA (AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average) + Ridge Regression. ARIMA learns the fund's own rhythm; Ridge Regression looks at recent momentum, volatility and the market. The two are blended, and the range widths are tuned so that past 80% ranges really held about 80% of the time.

Limitations
  • Short-term fund prices are mostly random. For many equity funds no model beats "stays the same".
  • It only knows the past. Sudden events (elections, rate changes, global shocks) can push the NAV outside both ranges.
  • About 1 time in 5 the NAV should land outside the 80% range, by design.
  • It never changes the weekly verdict and is not a reason to buy or sell.
