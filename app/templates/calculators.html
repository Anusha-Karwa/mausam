How these numbers are worked out

We turn the yearly return into a monthly one so a year of growth adds up to exactly the rate you typed: monthly rate = (1 + yearly rate)1/12 − 1. At 12% a year that is about 0.95% a month.

SIP instalments go in at the start of each month and grow for that month. The SWP takes the withdrawal out at the start of each month and the rest grows.

Real funds don't grow at a steady rate. They rise and fall, and the order of good and bad years matters, especially for withdrawals. These calculators ignore taxes, exit loads and the fund's {{ term('expense ratio', 'expense ratio') }} (which is already inside the NAV of a real fund). Use them to compare ideas, not to plan to the rupee.
